About me

My learning journey

My professional journey in Australia has provided me with valuable opportunities to understand children's learning and development across early childhood and primary school settings. Working as an educator at Kate Cocks Community Children's Centre has deepened my understanding of play-based learning and the importance of creating responsive, inclusive and engaging learning environments. Through everyday interactions, observations and collaborative experiences, I have come to recognise how children construct knowledge, develop relationships and express their ideas through play.

Building on this foundation, my professional placement in a Reception classroom at Kidman Park Primary School has strengthened my skills in intentional teaching, curriculum planning and assessment. This experience has helped me understand how purposeful teaching strategies, informed by children's individual learning needs and interests, can support their engagement and progress. It has also encouraged me to become more reflective and confident in making pedagogical decisions.

These experiences have shaped my emerging professional identity as an educator who values meaningful relationships, child-centred learning and intentional teaching. I believe that every child is a capable and unique learner who deserves to feel valued, supported and empowered in their learning journey.

This portfolio documents my professional growth, teaching experiences and developing pedagogical understanding. Through selected evidence and critical reflections, it demonstrates how I connect educational theory with practice and continue to develop my identity as a reflective and responsive teacher.
