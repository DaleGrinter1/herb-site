Values that guide my practice

My teaching philosophy

How I hope to help children feel connected, curious, and confident.

Belonging comes first

I value meaningful relationships and believe that every child deserves to feel valued, supported and empowered in their learning journey. These beliefs shape my emerging identity as a child-centred educator.

Learning through play

My work at Kate Cocks Community Children?s Centre has deepened my understanding of play-based learning. Everyday interactions, observations and collaborative experiences have helped me recognise how children construct knowledge, develop relationships and express their ideas through play. My Reception placement has also strengthened my understanding of intentional teaching and purposeful strategies informed by children?s needs and interests.

Every learner matters

I believe every child is a capable and unique learner. I aim to create responsive, inclusive and engaging learning environments, with teaching informed by children?s individual learning needs and interests.

Growing through reflection

My experiences across early childhood and primary settings have encouraged me to become more reflective and confident in making teaching decisions. Through selected evidence and critical reflection, I connect educational theory with practice and continue developing as a reflective and responsive teacher.
